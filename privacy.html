Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile apps that link here. Those apps do not ask you to create an account. They do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Questions: app.couchin@gmail.com

1. What stays on your device

Progress, such as the level you have reached, is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes that record.

The app creates a random install identifier on the device. It is not tied to your name.

2. Technical data sent to our server

To download content, artwork, and to check whether an update is required, an app may contact our server. Those requests can include:

  • App version
  • Platform, such as Android or iOS
  • Operating system version
  • Device manufacturer and model
  • Language setting
  • Install identifier

This information is used to run the app, update its content, and see which version is supported. No user account is created on the server.

3. Advertising

Apps may show ads through Google AdMob, including a banner and a full-screen ad at some moments in the app.

Google may process an advertising identifier, IP address, and whether an ad was shown, under its own policy. Personalized ads depend on your device’s ad settings.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Technical data is shared only with the server that runs the app and with Google when ads are shown. It may also be shared when the law requires it.

5. Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. How long data is kept

Progress stays on your device. Technical records sent to the server are kept only as long as needed to operate and protect the app, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Your choices

To ask for access, correction, or deletion, email app.couchin@gmail.com. The copy of the install identifier on your device is removed when you delete the app.

8. Changes

If this text changes, the date above changes with it. The current version is always this page.
