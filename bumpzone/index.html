How it plays

1

Choose the fight

Offline vs bots or an online room. Set team size — bots fill empty slots.

2

Knock them out

Shoot, dodge bushes, smash crates. A knocked-out rival drops the flag.

3

Capture

Carry the enemy cup to your pad. Defend yours until the timer hits zero.

Menus

Jump offline with bots, or queue an online room. Every map has its own trap — revolving doors, glass, rapids, mirrors, storm winds.

In the arena

Matches play landscape. Move with the stick, FIRE to shoot, SUPER when it lights up — then get the flag back to base.
